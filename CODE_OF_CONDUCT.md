# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project exists to help people genuinely understand the code they ship. That only works
in a space where asking a basic question is safe. We pledge to make participation in
NirmiqCodeSensei a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level,
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps this community:

- Being welcoming to beginners — "I don't understand this yet" is the point of this project
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback on code
- Focusing on what is best for users and the project
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Condescension about someone's skill level or choice of tools
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and
commit history — and when an individual is officially representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported privately
to the maintainer via GitHub (open a private security advisory, or contact
[@SheeshDarth](https://github.com/SheeshDarth)). All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or
permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive,
or harmful.
